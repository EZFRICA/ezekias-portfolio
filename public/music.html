Majina

Majina means “names” in Swahili. Vol. 1 is a roll call of West Africa, from Sundiata Keïta in the 13th century to Thomas Sankara. Its tracks started from MIDI seeds I wrote, humanised and voiced in Apple GarageBand, so the songs carry my chords instead of a generic prompt. I also designed its web player, where each name fills with brass as its track plays.

What the models taught me

Transcription is the test suite

A generated voice can sound right and sing the wrong word. Running Scribe and Gemini on the final audio, line by line, shows exactly where.

Some sounds still break

isiZulu clicks and Wolof vowels are where generation fails most. Phonetic spelling helps, and native speakers remain the last reviewers.

Prompts describe, never name

Music models refuse real artist and song names. A good prompt describes instruments, rhythm, voices and mood instead.

Your own input changes the output

A MIDI seed played in GarageBand gives the model a direction no text prompt can, and keeps an album from sounding like the same song twelve times.

Video is just code

Lyric videos written in HTML and rendered to MP4 can be corrected and re-rendered in minutes, in three languages.

Declare it

Every release is declared as made with AI on distribution. Listeners deserve to know, and it costs nothing.
